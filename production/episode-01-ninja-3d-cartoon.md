## Script, performance & shots
0–2s / HOOK. Camera tracks a starving HOONI racing toward the kitchen. His stomach growls like thunder. HOONI: '배고파! 지금 당장!'
2–8s / CHAOS. HOONI struggles to fit a mountain of frozen fries into the air fryer basket. Tight reaction shot: towering fries wobble; no dangerous fire or mishap. Funny spring sound. PIP watches silently.
8–14s / COMEDY. PIP: '잠깐! 너 지금 감자튀김이랑 전쟁 중이야?' HOONI looks straight at robot, incredulous. Basket springs open gently; harmless exaggerated fries dance through air.
14–23s / USEFUL TWIST. PIP waves a mini hologram with three icons: '용량', '조리 기능', '세척'. PIP: '에어프라이어는 크기만 보고 고르면 안 돼!' HOONI: '그럼 뭘 봐야 하는데?!'
23–31s / VISUAL PAYOFF. HOONI chases a runaway fry around counter. A single fry lands in PIP's robotic claw; PIP offers it, then comically retracts it. No unsupported specific brand claim.
31–37s / SUBSCRIBE VIA JOKE. HOONI and PIP both look at the camera. PIP: '다음 사고도 보고 싶다면 구독!' HOONI: '사고 아니거든!'
