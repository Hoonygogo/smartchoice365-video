# Video editorial standard

The aim is useful, original comparisons, not a guarantee of YPP acceptance.

- Give each video its own question, evidence, tradeoffs and reasoned verdict; changing a product name inside a generic template is insufficient.
- Separate manufacturer facts, our editorial interpretation, hypothetical examples and hands-on results. Do not invent tests or experience.
- Check exact model, variant and verification date. Narrow the claim to the item actually verified.
- Explain numbers and limitations, rather than reading marketing claims as personal guarantees.
- Show relevant original tables, accurate charts and decision examples. Animation alone does not make derivative content original.
- Provide an actionable conclusion inside the video. Website links supply extra evidence and tools.
- Keep AI lifestyle scenes illustrative; use “이해를 돕기 위한 연출 이미지” on screen. Preserve legally/platform-required disclosures, including the upload disclosure for realistic synthetic scenes.
- Match source links, captions and description to the exact approved video version. Public upload requires explicit user approval.
- Assess the channel as a whole for repetitive content, rights and monetization rules before applying. Subscriber/watch thresholds and a channel review remain necessary.
